# 07. Honorable Mentions

Creators whose free blogs, talks and videos hold a huge amount of knowledge, curated from what I actually follow.

- [ByteByteGo](https://bytebytego.com): system design
- [You Suck at Programming](https://www.youtube.com/@yousuckatprogramming) (YouTube): where I learnt Bash
- [ChiefGyk3D](https://github.com/ChiefGyk3D) (GitHub): security
- [NetworkChuck](https://networkchuck.com): home labbing and an intro to playing with hardware
- [Alta3 Research](https://www.youtube.com/@Alta3Research) (YouTube): Chad, the best instructor I learnt Kubernetes from; he simplifies complex topics, and he made Kubernetes click when it had been too complex to follow
- [freeCodeCamp.org](https://www.freecodecamp.org): Jenkins courses
- [ExamPro](https://www.exampro.co) (Andrew Brown): AWS and Azure content
- [DevOps Directive](https://devopsdirective.com): GitHub Actions
- [IBM Technology](https://www.youtube.com/@IBMTechnology) (YouTube): mainly AI content
- [Packt](https://www.packtpub.com): a library of courses and books on everything
