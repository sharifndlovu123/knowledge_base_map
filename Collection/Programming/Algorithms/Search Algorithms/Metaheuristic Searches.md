

Metaheuristics Characteristics
The following characterise metaheuristics
- Meta-heuristics algorithms are strategies that determine the path and direction of the search
process.
- Metaheuristics are usually stochastic.
- Meta-heuristics have a mechanism that enables them to escape local minima (maxima).
- Meta-heuristics are usually general and non-specific.
- May have domain knowledge in the lower level heuristics.

Some examples of 
Singlepoint searches
- Simulated Annealing
- Tabu Search
- Iterated Local Search 
- Variable Neighbourhood Search.
